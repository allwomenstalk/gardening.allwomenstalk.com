# 5 Easy Vegetables Kids Can Grow Indoors

I think letting kids start a garden of their own teaches them responsibility and how to take the initiative on a project of their very own. If you have an old aquarium on hand or can find one at a garage sale, be sure to pick it up. The 5 vegetables kids can grow indoors that are listed below can be planted in any type of container, but watching the roots spread throughout the dirt in an aquarium is a lot of fun for most kids.

Here are a few quick tips to set your indoor garden up for success:

- Use containers with good drainage holes to prevent root rot.
- Place your pots near a sunny window that gets plenty of bright light.
- Keep the soil consistently moist without overwatering.

## 5. Snow Peas

You only have to plant a single pea seed to get an entire plant of [snow peas](https://www.almanac.com/plant/peas). Kids love watching the vine snake up a narrow fence that is attached to a stake sunk into a 5 gallon bucket. Place the bucket in front of a sunlit window and the pea plant will take off all on its own.

## 4. Pole Beans

Pole beans can be set up in a similar way to the [snow peas](#5) plant. Again, as single bean is all that is needed to get these beans started. Learning how to stake pole beans with simple garden stakes gives kids a fun project, and you can help kids wind the bean plant around a window so that it continues to grow in the sunlight.

## 3. Eggplants

Since these plants don’t get extremely large, especially dwarf container varieties, they can be grown in a bucket of dirt with lots of success. Put some well-draining soil in a 5 gallon bucket, place a single plant in the middle and watch it grow. You can check out [eggplant growing guidelines](https://www.almanac.com/plant/eggplants) for best results.

| Vegetable | Container Type | Sunlight Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Snow Peas | 5-Gallon Bucket | Full Sun |
| Pole Beans | Window Bucket | Full Sun |
| Eggplants | 5-Gallon Bucket | Full Sun |
| Radishes | Shallow Container | Partial Sun |
| Carrots | Glass Aquarium | Full Sun |

## 2. Radishes

Radish seeds germinate in a short amount of time and can be placed in a shallow container, since they don’t grow very deep down into the soil. These veggies could also be grown in the same container as the [carrots](#1), if you like.

## 1. Carrots

These are a perfect veggie for growing along the edges of the aquarium. Kids enjoy seeing the bright orange root make its way deeper and deeper into the dirt. [Carrot seeds](https://www.almanac.com/plant/carrots) will grow in just about any type of soil and require very little care, which makes them perfect for a kid’s garden.

If you have a little kid who is interested in gardening, then maybe he/she will like growing some of these vegetables. Have you already tried helping your child grow any certain types of veggies in the house?
