# 5 Beautiful Plants for Your Garden Plot to Create Stunning Blooms

My flower garden is a source of great joy for me. I smile every time I see the brightly colored blooms. I also find taking care of these little beauties to be very relaxing, almost therapeutic. If you are looking to cultivate some of the prettiest plants in the world, having a thoughtfully curated garden plot makes all the difference. My top 5 beautiful plants for a garden plot are listed below. See what you think about these gorgeous plants and how they can transform your [flower garden](https://gardening.allwomenstalk.com/).

## 5. Black-eyed Susan

I like the color yellow, which is probably why I was drawn to this flower. These seem to bloom endlessly and really make a garden much more vibrant. If you pinch off the dried flowers when they finish blooming and sprinkle them around the existing plant, then this will speed up the process of getting this flower to spread. For detailed care instructions, check out the [Missouri Botanical Garden guide](https://www.missouribotanicalgarden.org/PlantFinder/PlantFinderDetails.aspx?taxonid=257463) on Rudbeckia hirta.

- **Light Needs:** Full sun
- **Care Tip:** Deadhead dried blooms to encourage spreading
- **Bloom Duration:** Summer through early fall

## 4. Zinnia Elegans

There are around 20 varieties of Zinnia, but these are my favorite. I love how full they look—each one reminds me of a mum. The seed mixes tend to be for a variety of colors, which is an easy way to add extra color to any garden. You can consult the [Royal Horticultural Society Zinnia tips](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/zinnia) for ideal planting conditions.

## 3. Lily of the Valley

Even though this is a tiny lily, the fragrance that comes off the miniature blossoms is amazing. These lilies don’t grow very tall, so they look great in front of taller plants. I have some planted in front of my [Blue Flag Irises](#2), which adds some design to the over-abundance of leaves on each iris.

| Plant Variety | Key Feature | Ideal Placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black-eyed Susan | Long-lasting yellow blooms | Garden borders & beds |
| Zinnia Elegans | Full, mum-like colorful heads | Sunny garden plots |
| Lily of the Valley | Fragrant sweet-scented bell flowers | In front of taller perennials |
| Blue Flag Iris | Dainty violet-blue petals | Moisture-rich areas |
| Stargazer Lily | Vibrant pink petals with white trim | Focal points in garden |

## 2. Blue Flag Iris

This is one of the daintier irises around. They are more purple in color than blue, and the leaves are narrower than regular irises. I first saw Blue Flag Irises along the lakeshore, but they do great in my flower garden as well. You can read more about native species on the [USDA Plant Database](https://plants.usda.gov/).

## 1. Stargazer Lily

I adore lilies in general, but the coloration of this one is the most amazing of them all. The pink is so vibrant and happy. I like the added touch provided by the darker speckles all over the petals that are each framed in a fine line of white. They pair wonderfully with other [beautiful plants](https://gardening.allwomenstalk.com/) in any home garden plot.

I think I got my love for plants from my parents. They have always shared their knowledge about gardening and allowed me to participate in the planting of gardens from the time I could walk.
